---
title: Why I hand over the source code
topic: Business
summary: Every client owns everything I write for them, from day one. It's the best sales argument I have, and it isn't a sales argument.
---

When I started, a mentor told me to keep the code and license it, so clients would keep paying. I understand the logic. I've never followed it.

## Lock-in is a tax on trust

A small business owner hiring a solo consultant is already taking a risk. If I also hold their operations hostage, the only reason they keep working with me is that leaving is expensive. That's not a relationship I want.

## It changes how I write code

Knowing someone else might read it next month, I write better documentation, fewer clever tricks and more tests. The code is better for it, and so is my own maintenance work years later.

## And it's good for business

Nearly every client who could leave, stays. Several have told me the source code clause is the reason they signed in the first place.
