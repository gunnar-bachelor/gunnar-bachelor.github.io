An identity for a practice whose buildings are defined by proportion.

## Approach

The logotype, stationery and a 240-page monograph all follow one proportional system taken from the studio's own floor plans.

> The grid is not a constraint. It is a way of making decisions visible.

## Outcome

The monograph was shortlisted for the Most Beautiful Swiss Books award.
