A festival identity built on a single geometric rule: one circle, set on a strict grid, scaled and repositioned for every day of the programme.

## Brief

Nordlicht needed an identity that worked across posters, tickets, signage and a programme website, and that could be produced quickly by a small in-house team.

## Approach

- A 12-column grid shared by every format, from Instagram tiles to 4-metre banners
- One typeface in two weights, and nothing else
- A colour system of black, white and one signal red

## Outcome

The team produced over 140 assets in-house in the first season. Ticket sales rose 30% on the previous year.
