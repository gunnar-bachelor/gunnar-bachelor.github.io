A new online shop and specimen site for an independent type foundry.

## Brief

Replace a slow template store with a site that shows the typefaces themselves, not screenshots of them.

## Approach

Every specimen is real, editable text. Visitors can type, resize and compare. The site is static, with checkout handled by a third-party provider, so it loads in under a second.

## Outcome

Conversion doubled in the first quarter after launch.
